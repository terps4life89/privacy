Lumi Mirrors — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Lumi Mirrors ("the game") is developed by Tiny Spark Lab, Poland ("we"). Contact: tinysparkgames@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, skins and settings are saved on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your game progress (stars, coins, beam and mirror skins, achievements, daily streak) is stored in your Google account using Play Games "Saved games", so you can continue on another phone. This data is stored by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd and is linked to your Play Games profile; we cannot see it. You can delete it in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account & data) or by signing out of Play Games. Google Play Games privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

With your permission, the game sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd): which levels are started, completed or restarted, whether hints were used, how often ads are shown, and coin and skin events. Firebase also records technical data such as an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, country derived from the IP address, and session length. We use this only to find levels that are too hard and to improve the game. We do not collect your name or e-mail, we do not use the advertising ID for statistics, and the data is not used for advertising.

  • Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked before any statistics are collected; nothing is sent until you agree (legal basis: consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • In other regions statistics are on by default (legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the game).
  • You can turn statistics off at any time in the game settings ("Statistics: on/off").
  • Firebase keeps event data for up to 14 months. Data may be processed in the USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Google's standard contractual clauses.

How Google uses data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd). To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device model and operating system version;
  • information about ad views and clicks, approximate location derived from the IP address.

Before any ads are requested, players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept or refuse personalised ads. If you refuse, ads may still be shown, but they are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game settings ("Privacy settings").

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications

If you agree, the game schedules one local reminder a day ("The new daily level is ready"). It is created on your device; nothing is sent to us or to third parties. You can turn reminders off in the game settings or in Android settings.

In-app rating

After some levels the game may open Google Play's in-app review window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; we only see them as public reviews.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to processing. Data stored by the game itself can be deleted by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings. For data processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You may lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (UODO, https://uodo.gov.pl).

Changes

We will publish any changes to this policy on this page.

Lumi Mirrors — Polityka prywatności (skrót)

Postępy w grze i ustawienia są przechowywane na urządzeniu, a po zalogowaniu do Gier Google Play — także jako kopia w „zapisanych grach” na Twoim koncie Google (zapis w chmurze; usuniesz go w aplikacji Gry Google Play). Za Twoją zgodą (w EOG pytamy przed wysłaniem czegokolwiek) gra wysyła anonimowe statystyki do Google Analytics for Firebase: które poziomy są rozpoczęte, ukończone lub nieudane i jak często wyświetlane są reklamy; bez imienia, e-maila i identyfikatora reklamowego. Przed wyświetleniem reklam w EOG pokazujemy formularz zgody (Google UMP) — zgodę na reklamy spersonalizowane można zmienić w ustawieniach gry. Przypomnienia o poziomie dnia są lokalnymi powiadomieniami na urządzeniu (można je wyłączyć w ustawieniach). Gra nie jest skierowana do dzieci poniżej 13 lat.
